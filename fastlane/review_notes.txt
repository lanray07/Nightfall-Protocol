Successful campaign missions unlock the next chapter; failure does not. Solo mission selection also offers all six mission types directly.

The six mission types now enforce different actions: memory decoding, slowed transport and five-second artifact stabilization, eight-second rift sealing with enemy surges and interruption, escorting a following echo, a thirty-second survival window with beacon activation, and two-terminal investigation followed by evidence recovery. Picking up optional loot does not advance mission tasks. Incomplete missions cannot bypass extraction requirements at high collapse.

Nightmare conditions now change play: blackout restricts visibility, Glass Maze relocates the exit more often, Hunted introduces an early-pursuing Hollow, Red Signal adds red false exits, Echo Trail follows a more recent player trail, and Zero Hour accelerates collapse. The campaign contains six connected briefings with saved chapter unlocking. Unfinished co-op/endless modes, nonfunctional loadout displays, and graphics-quality controls have been removed. The language selector exposes the complete English interface only. Audio and haptic controls now affect playback/feedback and persist their preferences.
