Successful campaign missions unlock the next chapter; failure does not. Solo mission selection offers all six mission types.

Mission types enforce different actions: memory decoding; slowed transport and five-second artifact stabilization; eight-second rift sealing with enemy surges and interruption; escorting a following echo; a thirty-second survival window with beacon activation; and two-terminal investigation followed by evidence recovery. Optional loot does not advance mission tasks. Incomplete missions cannot bypass extraction requirements at high collapse.

Conditions change play: blackout restricts visibility, Glass Maze relocates the exit more often, Hunted introduces an early-pursuing Hollow, Red Signal adds red false exits, Echo Trail follows a more recent player trail, and Zero Hour accelerates collapse.
