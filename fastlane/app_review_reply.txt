App Review Team,

iOS 1.0 (190) replaces build 175 with the Field Receiver revision. Attached: all nine answers in detail.

1. Nightfall is an offline, touch-controlled noncombat horror extraction game. Players complete mission tasks, evade entities and return before collapse, keeping carried loot only on success. The new receiver hides distant entities and optional loot. Sounding reveals them for four seconds, while entities investigate the emitted location for seven. The operator can leave that point as a decoy. Hollow marks remember heard positions without live tracking. Silent movement reduces speed to 60% and detection range to 55%, and blocks transmission. Charge returns in fourteen active seconds. The clock and entities start with the first movement or sounding, allowing safe preparation.

2. Intended users seek brief individual sessions of atmospheric horror, evasion, route planning and time pressure on iPhone/iPad, without multiplayer coordination, sign-in or constant connectivity. The guide and campaign introduce the rules.

3. Information now creates risk: the useful reveal ends before the entity investigation does. This operates alongside six separate mission rules: recover/decode, slowed artifact transport/stabilization, interrupted rift work, echo escorting, timed survival and two-terminal investigation. The niche we target combines that work with a visible floor plan, carried-loot loss, changing extraction signals and worsening collapse. Sound-driven horror is not itself a new genre; the report explains the combination with published comparisons. Original code-drawn coil artwork/icon, instrument console, contours, chambers and entity art replace the prior presentation.

4. Pure Swift receiver/mission checks and all twelve Python tooling tests passed. Native app-model safeguards, actual touch-control sounding/silence/recharge/extraction, legal links and local StoreKit purchase/restoration/expiry checks passed on BOTH iPhone and iPad simulators in run 39010985203. These are developer simulator checks, not external beta feedback or physical-device sandbox testing. We have no documented external beta-user examples to supply. The initial gameplay check exposed damage while reading controls; the departure gate and silent-route check address that finding.

5. This is standalone. Scrap Squad: Merge & Survive centers on robot fusion, squads, weapons, waves and bosses. Nightfall uses receiver information risk, noncombat station work, escorting, collapse and its own campaign/progression. The other utility/training/productivity products do not host this game. This submission is iOS only.

6. An entire second game could technically be embedded in another app, but it would add separate controls, content, mission state, presentation and persistence rather than extend that app's purpose. Nightfall is maintained as its own complete game.

7. No significant shared proprietary gameplay component was identified in the inspected projects. Earlier exact-file comparisons have a defined local scope, not proof against every partial reuse. The receiver is implemented in Nightfall's own scene/rules/view model, with no new shared account-app framework.

8. No external Swift package, nonstandard game SDK or shared third-party app/content library was identified in the project. This revision introduces no template package or imported art library; it uses Apple frameworks and the new receiver drawing code. Inspection does not certify the historical origin of every older fragment or asset.
