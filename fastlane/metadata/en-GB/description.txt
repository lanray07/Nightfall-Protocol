Enter the dream. Escape the nightmare.

Nightfall Protocol is a solo psychological extraction horror game. Enter unstable nightmare zones, complete randomized objectives, collect artifacts, avoid patrolling entities, and extract before the Reality Collapse meter reaches zero.

Manage health and sanity as collapse events intensify. Decide whether to risk another artifact or head for extraction. Successful runs earn XP and keep collected loot; failed runs lose temporary loot while preserving long-term progression. Base missions and the extraction loop are playable offline.

An optional Premium Pass auto-renews monthly through Apple. While active, it provides a monthly rotating player and trail palette, an extraction glow, and an expanded artifact lore display. It does not affect gameplay power, enemies, loot odds, or progression. Purchases and restoration require a network connection.
