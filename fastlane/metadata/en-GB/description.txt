Nightfall Protocol is an offline, single-player survival horror game set inside unstable nightmare zones. Complete extraction missions, avoid enemy patrols and recover dream artifacts before Reality Collapse ends your run.

NIGHTMARE EXTRACTION
Choose a mission, enter the zone and complete objectives under pressure. Recover memories, investigate black sites, seal nightmare rifts and reach extraction with your loot.

SURVIVE REALITY COLLAPSE
Watch your health, sanity and the rising collapse meter. Enemy encounters, room events and mission modifiers keep each run tense. Decide when to keep exploring and when to escape.

COLLECT AND PROGRESS
Earn operator XP from successful runs, build your artifact collection and discover mission lore. Review your extraction score, rank and run code, then share your recap.

PLAY OFFLINE
Core solo gameplay works without an account or internet connection. Explore daily nightmare missions and return for another extraction run.
