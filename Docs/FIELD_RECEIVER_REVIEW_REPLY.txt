Hello App Review Team,

We have made a further substantive revision after the rejection of build 175. The replacement is iOS 1.0 (190), the Field Receiver revision. This changes both gameplay decisions and the app's presentation. Please find updated answers below and genuine captures from the replacement source.

1. Nightfall is an offline, touch-controlled noncombat horror extraction game. Players complete station tasks, evade entities and return before collapse, retaining carried loot only on success. The new receiver hides distant threats and optional loot until heard. Sounding reveals them for four seconds, while entities investigate the emitted position for seven. Players can leave that location as a decoy. Hollow marks remember prior positions without live tracking. Silent movement reduces speed to 60% and detection range to 55%, and blocks transmission. Charge returns in fourteen active seconds. The clock and entities start with the first map movement or Sounding, allowing safe preparation. These rules apply alongside six distinct mission types and the saved campaign.

2. Intended users want individual, brief sessions of atmospheric horror, route planning and time pressure on iPhone/iPad without multiplayer coordination, sign-in or constant connectivity. The field guide and campaign introduce the mechanics. Core gameplay and progression operate offline.

3. The experience we target makes information itself risky: the action that reveals a threat also gives it a place to investigate, and the useful reveal expires before that investigation ends. The operator can divert pursuit, switch to slower silence, and plan from stale echo marks. This replaces build 175's continuously visible distant entities. The new fractured-coil title artwork/icon, instrument console, seismic contours, receiving chambers/reels and directional entity/operator art express that mechanic throughout the interface. They are original code-drawn designs, rather than a recoloring of the previous grid and cards. The intended niche is mission-led extraction with a persistent visible floor plan, carried-loot risk and receiver-based entity information, rather than primarily navigating an unseen maze or fighting through a runner course.

For comparison, ECHO: The Dark Is Listening's published listing emphasizes whisper/call illumination, resonators and sound-hunting enemies in level-based mazes (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/echo-the-dark-is-listening/id6797635978). Into the Dead's listing emphasizes a zombie runner with weapons, perks and canine companions (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/into-the-dead/id535609266). Sound-driven horror is therefore not itself a new category. Nightfall's proposed contribution is the concrete combination of recover/decode, slowed transport, interrupted rift work, echo escorting and investigation with an emitted-location decoy and worsening collapse. This comparison describes published positioning, not exhaustive hands-on testing or proof that competitors lack every similar feature.

4. Pure Swift receiver/mission checks and all twelve Python tooling tests passed. Native app-model, actual touch-control receiver/extraction, legal-link and local StoreKit purchase/restoration/expiry checks passed on both iPhone and iPad simulators in https://github.com/lanray07/Nightfall-Protocol/actions/runs/39010985203. The tested shipping sources match build 190, which was signed, uploaded and processed as VALID. No external beta feedback is documented and we cannot supply beta-user examples. We distinguish developer simulator and local StoreKit checks from external beta feedback and physical-device sandbox testing. The current revision adds executable checks for transmission cost, recharge, silence, detection distance, reveal/pursuit timing and invalid elapsed time, plus real touch-control evidence of sounding, silence and successful extraction.

5. Nightfall is standalone, not a related-app suite. Scrap Squad: Merge & Survive uses robot fusion, weapons, squads, waves and bosses. Nightfall uses evasion, receiver information risk, station work, echo escorting, collapse and its own campaign/progression. The other utility/training/productivity account apps do not provide this game. Other Nightfall platform entries are part of this same app record; this submission is iOS only.

6. An entire second game could technically be embedded in another app. That would introduce separate gameplay, controls, mission state, content, presentation and persistence rather than extend Scrap Squad's combat systems or a utility app's purpose. Nightfall is maintained as its own complete game for this reason.

7. No shared proprietary gameplay module was identified in the inspected projects. The prior response disclosed the exact-file comparison scope and its limits; we do not claim that it certifies the absence of every form of partial reuse. This revision implements the receiver in Nightfall's own scene/rules/view model and introduces no shared account-app framework.

8. No external Swift packages, nonstandard game SDK, third-party app codebase or content library was identified in the inspected project. The revision adds no template package or imported artwork library. It uses standard Apple frameworks and the new checked-in receiver drawing code. Source inspection does not independently certify the historical origin of every pre-existing fragment or asset.
